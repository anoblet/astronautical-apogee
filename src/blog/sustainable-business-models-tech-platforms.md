Sustainability ensures long-term success:

- Explore open-source monetization (sponsorships, premium features)
- Differentiate services for diverse customer needs
- Build partnerships and community support

**Actionable Steps:**

- Offer tiered pricing and value-added services
- Engage with sponsors and contributors
- Track metrics for growth and retention

**Business Outcome:** Resilient revenue streams and lasting impact.
